FAQ

Common questions.

What marketplaces do you work with?+

The core focus is Walmart Seller Center and TikTok Shop.

Do you work with existing stores?+

Yes. The audit and service workflow can be used for existing stores and new projects.

What is included in a store audit?+

The audit is designed to identify priority issues, clarify next steps and define practical scope.

Can you handle one-off projects?+

Yes. One-off work is supported alongside ongoing operational support.

Do you provide ongoing store management?+

Yes. Store Management is one of the nine primary services.

Do you guarantee sales or revenue?+

No. Sales and revenue are not guaranteed claims.

How long does a typical project take?+

Timelines depend on scope, but typical ranges are: Product Research and Compliance Review, 2–4 days; Product Sourcing, 3–5 days; Listing + Marketplace SEO and Product Graphics, 2–4 days; TikTok Shop setup, 2–3 days; Walmart Store Setup, 7–14 days of active work (Walmart’s own account verification runs separately and can take 1–4 weeks, outside freelancer control); Customer Support workflow setup, 3–5 days. Store Management is ongoing and billed weekly or monthly rather than as a one-time delivery. Exact timelines are confirmed during the Free Store Audit once project scope is clear.

How does pricing work?+

Pricing is scoped individually for each project rather than fixed to a single public rate, since a single-listing fix, a full store setup and ongoing management all carry different amounts of work. Factors include platform, store size, service combination and whether the work is one-off or ongoing. Exact pricing and payment structure are shared directly after the Free Store Audit, once the scope is clear.
