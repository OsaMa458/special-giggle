FAQ

Common questions.

Do you work with US and UK sellers?

Yes. The core focus is Walmart Seller Center and TikTok Shop workflows for sellers who need research, setup, optimization or ongoing support.

Can you set up a new store?

Yes, depending on the platform, account status and the exact setup scope. A short consultation is used to confirm what can be handled.

Do you provide product research and sourcing?

Yes. Product research covers demand, competition and economics, while sourcing focuses on supplier comparison, landed cost and practical supplier selection.

Do you guarantee sales or revenue?

No. Store performance depends on the product, price, competition, traffic, advertising, fulfillment and other factors. I focus on the work within the agreed scope.

Are the portfolio projects real client results?

No. Demonstrations are clearly labeled. Verified client case studies will only be added when permission and supporting evidence are available.
